# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end Data Analytics project that analyzes customer shopping behavior using **Python**, **SQL**, and **Power BI**. The project focuses on data cleaning, business analysis, KPI generation, and interactive dashboard development to derive actionable business insights.

---

# 📌 Problem Statement

Businesses need to understand customer purchasing behavior to improve customer experience, increase sales, and make data-driven decisions.

This project analyzes customer demographics, purchasing habits, product categories, subscription behavior, shipping preferences, and customer reviews to uncover valuable business insights.

---

# 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains approximately **3,900 customer records** with information such as:

# 📈 Key Business Insights

- Clothing category generated the highest revenue.
- Young Adult customers contributed the highest sales.
- Average Purchase Amount is **$59.76**.
- Average Review Rating is **3.75**.
- Subscription customers represent a significant portion of total purchases.
- Clothing products have the highest sales volume.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase inventory for Clothing products.
- Launch loyalty programs for non-subscribed customers.
- Promote Express Shipping for premium customers.
- Target marketing campaigns based on customer age groups.
- Improve promotional strategies using customer purchase behavior.
